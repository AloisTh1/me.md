# Exercise and Mood Correlation

I've observed that on days when I engage in at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise (brisk walking, cycling, or strength training), I experience:

- Improved mood throughout the day
- Reduced anxiety symptoms
- Better stress resilience

This pattern has been consistent over several months of tracking.
